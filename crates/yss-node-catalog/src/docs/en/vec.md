# VEC Fit

Connect at least two aligned finite I(1) series in time order. Configure cointegration rank, levels lag order lags, and deterministic specification trend (none, constant or trend).

Output model retains cointegrating and adjustment coefficients, short-run estimates, inference, design and residuals from the Johansen VEC estimator. VEC Summary projects these facts and computes residual LM or stability analyses only when selected, without refitting the model. No single-series fitted/residual output is provided.
